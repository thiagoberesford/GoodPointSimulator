Acronym & term dictionary

💰 Financial metrics

LTVLifetime Value — total revenue expected from one customer over their entire relationship with GoodPoint.
CACCustomer Acquisition Cost — what you spend on sales and marketing to win one new customer.
LTV:CACRatio of lifetime value to acquisition cost. SaaS benchmark is 3× or above.
ROIReturn on Investment — gain relative to what was spent, expressed as a percentage.
ARRAnnual Recurring Revenue — predictable, subscription-based revenue projected over a year.
MRRMonthly Recurring Revenue — same as ARR but expressed per month (ARR ÷ 12).
COGSCost of Goods Sold — direct cost per script delivered (LLM inference + infrastructure).
GMGross Margin — revenue minus COGS as a percentage. GoodPoint target: 90–95%.
Op. ProfitOperating Profit — what remains after subtracting both COGS and fixed costs from revenue.
Break-evenThe exact script volume where total revenue equals total costs — neither profit nor loss.
PaybackMonths until a customer's revenue recoups the cost of acquiring them. Target: ≤ 12 months.
ChurnPercentage of customers who stop using the product each month. Lower is better.
ppPercentage point — an absolute change in a percentage (e.g. churn falling from 3% to 2% = 1 pp reduction).
moMonth — used in "/mo" (per month) and "24 mo" (24 months).

📦 Pricing & sales

B2BBusiness to Business — selling to organisations (universities, departments) rather than individuals.
SaaSSoftware as a Service — subscription-based software delivered over the internet.
PLGProduct-Led Growth — users discover and adopt the product themselves, reducing reliance on sales.
GTMGo-to-Market — the strategy for how a product reaches its target customers.
ACVAnnual Contract Value — total value of a customer contract in a 12-month period.
NRRNet Revenue Retention — revenue retained from existing customers including expansions, minus churn.
PPUPrice Per Unit — revenue earned per single script processed.
Land & expandSales strategy: enter at departmental level (low friction), grow to faculty, then institutional licence.
Pilot tierFree or subsidised entry offer (0–500 scripts) used to build trust before converting to paid.
FreemiumModel where basic use is free and users pay for premium features or higher volume.
Usage-basedPricing tied to consumption — GoodPoint's per-script model. Revenue scales with volume.
LicenceFixed annual fee for unlimited or capped access — Faculty (£6–10K) or Institutional (£18–30K+).
FreemiumModel where core use is permanently free (ATA exam creation) to drive adoption, with premium features (AI grading) behind a paywall.
ATAArtificial Teaching Assistant — GoodPoint's exam/assessment creation tool offered free as the freemium entry point to the platform.
PQLProduct Qualified Lead — a free user whose in-product behaviour signals they are ready to convert to paid (e.g. created 3+ exams, invited colleagues).
Two-sided rewardReferral structure where both the referrer (professor who referred) AND the referee (new professor) receive free script credits. Increases acceptance rate vs. one-sided.
Viral coefficient (k)Average new customers generated by each existing customer through referrals. k = invites/customer × conversion rate. k ≥ 1 = viral loop. Dropbox achieved 0.7.
Blended CACWeighted average acquisition cost across all channels — direct sales, freemium conversion, and referrals. Referral programs reduce blended CAC by 25–35% (Forrester).
PLG flywheelSelf-reinforcing loop: Free ATA → professor trusts product → converts to paid grading → refers colleagues → more free ATA users → repeat.

🛠️ Technical & regulatory

LLMLarge Language Model — the AI model (e.g. GPT-4, Claude) GoodPoint uses to grade scripts. Main variable cost driver.
PWAProgressive Web App — a website that behaves like a native app. No app store needed. Best fit for GoodPoint now.
React NativeMeta's cross-platform framework — one codebase builds both iOS and Android apps.
FlutterGoogle's cross-platform framework — alternative to React Native, known for consistent UI.
GDPRGeneral Data Protection Regulation — EU/UK data privacy law governing how student data can be processed.
DPIAData Protection Impact Assessment — legal requirement before a university can process student data with a new third-party tool. Key procurement friction point.
ITTInvitation to Tender — formal procurement process triggered above a contract value threshold (~£25K in UK HE).
APIApplication Programming Interface — how GoodPoint connects to LLMs, LMS platforms, and other services.
LMSLearning Management System — university platforms like Canvas, Blackboard, or Moodle. Integration needed for institutional sales.
MVPMinimum Viable Product — the smallest version of the product that delivers core value and can be tested with real users.
CDNContent Delivery Network — serves files (like Chart.js) fast to any browser via distributed servers worldwide.

🎓 Market & sector

HEHigher Education — the university sector. GoodPoint's primary target market.
K-12Kindergarten to Grade 12 — primary and secondary school sector. Less relevant for GoodPoint.
SMBSmall and Medium-sized Business — used to describe smaller competitor segments (e.g. CoGrader, EduSageAI).
STEMScience, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics — GoodPoint's core differentiation: handwritten STEM grading.
CAGRCompound Annual Growth Rate — EdTech market growing at 14% CAGR 2025–2032.

🌱 How mobile amplifies Growth Mechanics

Freemium ATA × Mobile
A PWA gives professors instant access to the ATA exam creation tool from any device — no install barrier. Mobile push notifications can trigger freemium-to-paid conversion at peak moments (e.g. when an exam is due). Conversion 2.1× higher when in-app triggers combine with notifications (Mixpanel 2025).
Referral Program × Mobile
Mobile native share sheet makes referral link sharing frictionless — professors can share directly to WhatsApp, email, or Teams in one tap. Real-time credit balance visible in the app creates ongoing engagement. Mobile referral share rates: 25–35% vs ~12% on desktop (OpenView 2025).
